Simplifying the Rail Travel Experience

Travella is an all-in-one train schedule app built around the newly launched Lagos–Abeokuta–Ibadan train system. It brings accurate schedules, station details, real-time updates, and ticket information into one accessible place — solving a gap the official channels hadn't solved yet.

Designing Travella meant addressing the reality of first-time train travelers on a brand-new rail line, where accurate schedules, station details, and pricing were scattered across calls, hearsay, and outdated pages. The goal was to give people one dependable, user-friendly place to plan a train journey from start to finish.

Outcome

One dependable place for a brand-new rail line.

Travella brings train schedules, station details, real-time updates, and ticket pricing for the Lagos–Abeokuta–Ibadan line into one app — replacing calls, scattered searches, and outdated pages with a single user-friendly source, while staying flexible enough to extend to other rail systems.

Learning

The best answer to information chaos was structure, not more content.

  • Most of the friction wasn't a design problem — it was the absence of a single trustworthy source, so naming that gap precisely shaped every decision after.
  • Small features that came directly from research findings — real-time updates, in-app ticket pricing — earned their place far more than anything invented up front.
